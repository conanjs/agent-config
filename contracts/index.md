# Contracts index

The fifteen cross-repo contracts of the split (spec 022, AC-1.1). Each links to its folder: `schema.json` (JSON Schema 2020-12), `README.md` (rules, worked examples, missing or stale behaviour) and `examples/` (valid and invalid files that the parity test runs through both the schema and the reader, DR-103). The component list and the lock share one row because they are two files of one decision (DR-403).

**Rules for every contract.** The id is `name/vN`. A component pins the major only (FR-006). A reader accepts the current major and the previous one. Adding an optional key is not a new major; removing or renaming a key, or tightening a rule, is. A major bump follows DR-418: the reader first, then providers, then consumers, and the previous major is dropped when `doctor` shows no user. Unknown keys warn and are ignored (CON-10). Compatibility is diffed against the last `contracts/v...` tag on every commit (FF-6).

## In-repo interfaces

These belong to agent-config's installer, not to the cross-repo contracts: they are not counted in the fifteen. They are listed in `INTERFACES.md` with a usage spec and JSON Schemas (CON-15, CON-16); the sketch is [`installer-cli.md`](../specs/022-repo-split/contracts/installer-cli.md).

| Interface                                                | Owner        | Consumers                                                           | Versioning rule                                                                                                                      | Missing or stale                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| install-report/v1 (the `--json` output of the installer) | agent-config | V, coding agents and scripts that run `install`, `plan` or `doctor` | Versioned by its `schema:` key. Removing or renaming a field, or changing an exit code, is a new major; a new field is not (DR-418). | Not stored: every run prints a fresh report. A consumer that finds an unknown major stops and reads the usage text.                         |
| install-state/v1 (`{{AGENT_HOME}}/install-state.json`)   | agent-config | installer, `doctor`, uninstall                                      | Versioned by its `schema:` key. A newer major is refused with exit 1 and never rewritten (DR-205).                                   | Missing: nothing is installed yet and `install` creates it at its commit point. A pending journal is restored by `doctor` (DR-104, DR-202). |
